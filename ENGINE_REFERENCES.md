# Engine baseline references

References checked 11 November 2026. The named engines are swaps into the same
game coupe, not full reproductions of the donor vehicles.

- Toyota, 75-Year History, 1993 Supra JZA80: 2JZ-GTE, 2,997 cc, 280 PS and a
  six-speed manual on RZ. 280 PS is approximately 276 mechanical hp.
  https://www.toyota.co.jp/jpn/company/history/75years/vehicle_lineage/car/id60006850/index.html
- Toyota, Supra catalogue history: 2JZ-GTE, 280 PS / 44.0 kgf m (approximately
  432 Nm; specification depends on market/year).
  https://toyota.jp/ucar/catalog/brand-TOYOTA/car-SUPRA/
- Nissan, Skyline model-change release, 28/29 August 2000: RB25DET five-speed
  manual torque increased to 363 Nm at 3,200 rpm. RB25 gameplay baseline is
  modelled after the later NEO engine, not every RB25 variant.
  https://global.nissannews.com/ja-JP/releases/gt-r?origin=channel-NNG243
- Mazda, 15 December 1998 RX-7 release: 13B-REW twin rotor, 280 PS at 6,500 rpm
  for Type R/RS, five-speed manual. Approximately 276 mechanical hp.
  https://newsroom.mazda.com/en/publicity/release/1998/9812/981215be.html
- Chevrolet Performance, LS9: 6.2 L supercharged V8, 638 hp at 6,500 rpm and
  604 lb-ft at 3,800 rpm (approximately 819 Nm).
  https://www.chevrolet.com/ownercenter/content/dam/gmownercenter/gmna/dynamic/manuals/2009/chevrolet/corvette/2009_chevrolet_corvette_owners.pdf

No precise real-world identity was verified for the sample bank named ZB or the
Street recording. Both are explicitly labelled custom builds.

The complete torque curves, rev-limit settings, boost pressures, spool rates,
flywheel inertias, engine-braking values and tuned builds are gameplay design
choices. They should not be read as measured manufacturer calibration data.
The per-engine gearbox ratios are representative configurations; they can be
edited independently of the engine. Sound-bank pitch reference RPMs are also
playback approximations rather than verified source-recording RPM metadata.

V3.1 adds VQ35Recorded and MustangRecorded as real-car sound options with
representative gameplay power, torque, gearing and response values. Their tune
figures are design choices, not source-car dyno data or verified factory
calibrations. The audio sources, creators, licenses and edits are documented
in Drift Drive/audio/CREDITS.md.
