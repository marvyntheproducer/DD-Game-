# Cruise Life radio — original music

Three original algorithmic instrumental loops created for this game:

- Sakura FM — After Hours (lo-fi-inspired)
- Shiosai Air — Slow Tide (ambient)
- Night Lane — Quiet Corners (jazz-inspired)

No third-party songs, melodies or music samples are used. Synthesized keys,
pads, bass and percussion are generated by `tools/generate-cruise-radio.py`.
All three loops are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Each loop is approximately 53.33 seconds, mono 22,050 Hz Vorbis. They are also
embedded in `cruise-radio.js` for direct-file playback. `manifest.json` records
SHA-256 hashes. Radio defaults to Off. Imported personal music is played from
a local browser object URL; it is never uploaded or included in this project.
